RSN operates a legally bound Dual-Engine Model: market-rate commercial enterprise verticals (Solar EPC & Microgrids, CSR Project Implementation, E2E Data Collection & Analysis, Managed 360 Marketing & Sales, Campaign Design & Management, Telecom, Automation, Security, IT, and Real Estate) compete on technical merit under strict commercial SLAs, and 100% of net profits are retained within Uttar Pradesh to fund grassroots healthcare, education, animal welfare, clean energy livelihood financing, and environmental stewardship.
